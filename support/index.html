❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock the Pro version?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Unlock Pro". The purchase is processed securely through Apple's App Store. It's a one-time payment with no subscription.

Can I use Morse Trail offline?

Yes! Morse Trail works entirely offline. No internet connection is required to use any feature of the app.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

I purchased Pro but features are not unlocked. What should I do?

Try tapping "Restore Purchases" in the Settings screen. If the issue persists, restart the app or reinstall it. If you still have problems, contact us at bigcycles.app@gmail.com.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the app collect any personal data?

No. Morse Trail does not collect, store, or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is Morse Trail available for iPad?

Yes, Morse Trail supports both iPhone and iPad.
