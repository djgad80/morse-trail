Privacy Policy

Morse Trail — Last updated: June 2026

📋 Overview

Morse Trail is a Morse code learning app developed by Big Cycles. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect (and what we do not collect) when you use our app.

📦 Data We Collect

Morse Trail does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app works entirely offline. No account is required to use any feature of the app.

📱 Device & Usage Data

We do not collect any analytics, crash reports, or usage data from your device. We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs.

💳 Purchases

If you choose to unlock the Pro version, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information. Purchase records are managed by Apple under their privacy policy.

🔒 Cookies & Tracking

The app does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or any tracking technologies. This website (the support page) does not use cookies either.
